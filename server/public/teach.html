🧠 Our own brain · సొంత మెదడు

A free, open AI model (Google Gemma) that runs on this server. It answers when Claude can't: Claude is down, there is no key, or today's budget is used up. It reads the news, Wikipedia, the weather and what you teach here before answering. ఇది మన సర్వర్‌లోనే ఉండే సొంత మెదడు, ఏ కంపెనీ మీదా ఆధారపడదు.


      
      
      
      

    


    

      
📚 Learned answers · నేర్చుకున్న జవాబులు

      
Habit learns from Claude as it talks: when Claude answers a general question that is right for anyone (never anything about a person), the answer is kept here for 90 days, so Habit can answer it by itself when Claude can't (for example when this month's limit is used up). Delete any answer you don't want. 

      
When 3 or more different people ask the same general question, an answer is prepared at night (with Claude and the internet) and kept here. The same question is then answered at once, and our own brain uses it when Claude can't answer. Only the question is counted, never who asked; questions about a person, about today, or with long numbers are never kept. Delete any answer you don't like.

      📄 File skills · ఫైల్ నైపుణ్యాలు
      
      
      How Habit makes letters and office papers by itself when Claude can't (this month's limit is used up, no credit, Claude is down, or a person's free messages for today are used up). It starts with 19 ready formats: leave letter, applications, complaints, certificates, bank and school letters; office letters, reports, memos, endorsements, notices, show-cause notices, proceedings, covering letters, replies to petitions, office orders, minutes and Excel statements. Every time Claude makes a Word file, Habit learns that kind of file as a clean template: the format and the wording, with the person's details taken out (a file that still has a name or a number in it is not kept). Delete any you don't want.

        🎓 Teach Gemma · జెమ్మాకి నేర్పించు
        
      
        Habit's own model, habit-gemma: Google's open Gemma 4 (Apache 2.0), taught to talk and help like Habit, in Telugu and English. Its teachers are Big Gemma (on a free Kaggle GPU), your lessons here, and people's 👍 / 👎. Claude's words never teach it (Anthropic's terms don't allow it): not Claude's answers, not the answers learned from Claude, not documents Claude read. హ్యాబిట్ సొంత మోడల్‌కి పెద్ద జెమ్మా, మీరు, ప్రజల 👍/👎 నేర్పిస్తారు.               📰 Media posts · మీడియా పోస్టులు
              
      
              All day, new stories come from these news feeds. The server opens each story, and our own brain writes a short Telugu post from its facts with a link to it (Claude writes them when our own brain can't, up to a limit a day). Stories that many papers report come first. Every morning Claude also writes a few posts per section, and the server adds the panchangam (4 AM), weather warnings for farmers (6 AM and 6 PM) and mandi prices (2 PM, needs DATA_GOV_KEY).
              
      
              
      
              
      
              Where the posts come from
              
      
              Tap a section to see its feeds. ✓ = working (stories in it), ✗ = not working right now (it is tried again later). Remove the ones you don't want; add any newspaper's or TV channel's RSS feed address.

                🎬 Habit cartoons (our own maker) · హ్యాబిట్ కార్టూన్లు
                
      
                Our own cartoons, made fully on our server for free (no paid AI, no GPU): our own brain writes a tiny screenplay (a joke, riddle, fact, kids story or good thought) and Habit's cute characters act it out (elbows, knees, faces and the camera all move): Chai, Idli, Mango, the Habit puppy, Ammamma, Tatayya, the teacher, a cat and a parrot, in a tea shop, market, village, farm, school, on stage and more, with bubbles, voices, sounds and music. It also reads today's Media posts for fresh ideas. It learns by itself: the kinds people like (likes, comments, views) are made more often. People can also make their own in the app (Media › 🎬 Make a cartoon).
                
      
                
      
                
      
      
      
      
                
      
                  
      
      
      
      
      
                  
    
                  

    
                  
      
                  🤖 AI videos · AI వీడియోలు
                  
      
                  Every day Google's Gemini video model makes short videos for Media: a devotional one at 5 AM, and a kids story, health tip or farming tip at 4 PM. Only safe, timeless topics (never news or real people), with Telugu words on the video and a clear "AI-made" label. It needs GEMINI_API_KEY with billing on, and it never spends more than the monthly limit.
                  
      
                  
      
                    
      
                    Limits (you can change them)
                    
      
                    The server never spends more than the monthly money limit (Google charges about ₹6 for each second of AI video). People's own AI cartoons are free for them; when today's number is used, the app tells them to come back tomorrow (they can still choose the simple puppy cartoon). You, the owner (Team password saved in the app), have no daily limit; the monthly money limit still holds.
