Community rules and child safety

BigD AI (బిగ్ డి) · Last updated: 1 November 2026

The Media tab in BigD AI lets people share posts, photos, links and comments with everyone. To keep it safe and friendly for all ages, everyone who posts agrees to these rules.

Rules · నియమాలు

  • Be kind and respectful. No insults, bullying, hate or threats against anyone.
  • No nudity, sexual content, or violent or gory pictures.
  • Don't share anyone's phone number, address, Aadhaar, PAN, bank or card details, or private chats.
  • No spam, advertisements, scams, fake news, or payment QR codes.
  • Post only photos and videos you are allowed to share. Videos from other apps are shared as links.
  • Nothing illegal under Indian law.

How we keep it safe

  • Every photo and every post or comment is checked automatically before it appears. Posts with phone numbers, ID numbers or bad content are not shown.
  • Anyone can report a post or comment (⋯ › Report) and hide posts from a person. Posts reported by several people are hidden until the team checks them.
  • The BigD team removes posts that break these rules and blocks people who keep breaking them.
  • You can delete your own posts and comments at any time.

Child safety standards

BigD AI has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM). Such content is never allowed. Photos are checked before they are shown, any such content found is removed at once, the account is blocked, and we report it to the authorities as Indian law requires (for example through the National Cybercrime Reporting Portal, cybercrime.gov.in). We cooperate with law enforcement on child safety matters.

To report a concern about a child's safety in the app, use Report in the app or contact the child safety contact below.
