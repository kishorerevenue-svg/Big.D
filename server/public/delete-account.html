Delete your BigD AI profile

మీ BigD AI ప్రొఫైల్‌ను తొలగించండి · App: BigD AI (బిగ్ డి) · Developer: Kishore Kumar

You can delete your Media profile in BigD AI at any time. This deletes your profile and everything linked to it, for ever. You don't need to do anything else: the rest of the app works without a profile.

1. In the app (the easy way)

  1. Open BigD AI and go to the Media · మీడియా tab.
  2. Tap your round picture at the top, then ✏️ Edit profile.
  3. At the bottom, tap 🗑 Delete my profile, then Delete.

You can also find it in the app on the Pet page › Privacy › Delete my Media profile.

2. Here, with your backup code

Your backup code is in the app: Media › your profile › 🔑 Backup code. It was also shown when you made your profile.

3. No app and no code?

Email bigdai.new@gmail.com with your name and @name in BigD AI. We may ask you a question to make sure the profile is yours, and then delete it within 7 days.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, @name, tagline, profile photo and backup code.
  • All your posts and the photos in them, and all comments on your posts.
  • Your comments on other posts, your likes, the people you follow, and your reports.
  • The link between your phone and the profile.
  • When you delete from the app: also your old posts and likes from the earlier Community page.

What is kept

  • Nothing that says who you are. Copies inside our automatic nightly backups are deleted within 7 days.
  • View counts on other people's posts stay as plain numbers (they don't say who viewed).
  • If the BigD team had stopped your profile for breaking the community rules, we keep a scrambled code of your phone, so the same phone can't simply start posting again.

Things BigD AI saves only on your phone (reminders, notes, your Vault, what the pet remembers) are not on our server; they are removed when you uninstall the app. See the privacy policy.
