BigD AI Privacy Policy

BigD AI (బిగ్ డి) is a chat app with an AI cartoon character that helps you understand, translate and write messages in Telugu and English. This page explains what happens to your information.

What we collect

We do not ask for your name, phone number, email or location, and the app has no login.

When you send a message, the text of your message and the recent conversation is sent to our server and then to Anthropic's Claude AI service so it can write a reply. If you tap "Report" on a reply, we save that reply, your question and your reason so we can improve the app.

We do not store your conversations on our server.

Our server keeps short-lived technical records (such as your internet address) only to stop misuse and too many requests.

Who processes your messages

Replies are generated by Claude, an AI service from Anthropic. Anthropic processes messages under its own commercial terms and privacy policy and does not use API messages to train its models by default. Read more at anthropic.com/legal/privacy.

Voice and microphone

The app uses the microphone only while you tap the puppy to talk. Your phone's own voice-typing service turns your speech into text; we receive only that text, never audio. Replies are read aloud by your phone's text-to-speech engine.

Natural voice

If natural voice is on, the text the pet speaks out loud (its replies and reminders, and the messages it reads aloud if you turn on "Read new messages aloud") is sent from our server to Google Cloud Text-to-Speech to create the spoken audio. Your own voice recordings are never sent. The audio files are kept on our server for up to 7 days so repeated phrases play faster, then deleted. You can switch natural voice off in the app; the pet then uses your phone's own voice.

Photos

If you choose a photo to ask about, a smaller copy is sent through our server to Anthropic's Claude service to answer your question. We do not store your photos.

Lists, notes, reminders and memories

Your to-do list, notes, reminders and the things the puppy remembers about you are saved only on your phone. With each message, the app sends them to our server so the AI can use them in its answer. We do not store them on our server. You can delete any of them in the app at any time.

Contacts

If you allow Contacts, the app looks up a phone number on your phone when you ask it to call or message someone. Your contacts are never sent to our server.

Internet search

For questions about current events, prices or other recent information, the AI may search the web. The search is done by Anthropic's service using words from your question; links to the sources are shown with the answer.

Message replies (WhatsApp, SMS and other chat apps)

This feature is off until you turn it on and give the app "Notification access" in your phone's settings. When it is on, the app reads new message notifications only from the chat apps you choose in the app (for example WhatsApp or Messages). For each new message, the sender's name, the chat name and the message text are sent to our server and to Anthropic's Claude service to write a suggested reply. Nothing is sent to the other person until you tap Send, unless you switch on Auto-reply.

If you switch on Auto-reply, the app replies by itself to new messages from the chosen apps, based on the auto-reply message you write, and stops when you switch it off. It does not auto-reply in group chats and replies at most once per chat every 30 minutes. We do not store your messages or replies on our server. You can turn the feature off in the app, or remove Notification access in your phone's settings, at any time.

Reading your screen (only when you ask)

If you tap "Read my screen" or ask the pet about your screen, Android first asks your permission ("Start capturing?"). Only after you agree, the app takes one picture of your screen, and a smaller copy is sent through our server to Anthropic's Claude service to answer your question. The picture is deleted from the phone right after it is sent, and we do not store it on our server. The app never records your screen continuously. Screens that block screenshots, such as banking apps, password screens and the app's own Vault, appear black.

Hands-free control (BigD AI Plus only)

The Plus edition can use Android's Accessibility service, only if you turn it on after reading an explanation in the app. It performs a fixed action when you ask for it: when you say a command such as "next reel", "pause", "go back" or "go home", or tap one of the pet's remote buttons, it performs exactly that one swipe, tap or button press. It does not read, collect, store or send the content of your screen, and the AI never decides to act on its own. In "Reels voice mode" the pet keeps listening only for the words next, previous, pause and like; what you say is turned into text by your phone's voice typing service, and the words are checked on your phone only, never sent to our server. You can switch it off at any time in your phone's Accessibility settings.

Floating on your screen

With your permission the puppy appears over other apps. Apart from the message notifications described above, the app cannot see or read what other apps show.

Children

BigD AI is not directed at children under 13.

AI replies can be wrong

BigD AI is an AI and can make mistakes, including in translations. Please check important information before you rely on it.

Uninstalling the app removes everything it saved on your phone. To ask us to delete any reports or stored messages, email [YOUR EMAIL].
