Habit AI Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026 · App owner: Kishore Kumar · Contact: bigdai.new@gmail.com

Habit AI (హ్యాబిట్) is a chat app with an AI cartoon character that helps you understand, translate and write messages in Telugu, English and Hindi, and helps with reminders, letters, government services and emergencies. This page explains what happens to your information.

What we collect

You don't need an account to use Habit AI. A login is only for your Media profile, and only if you choose to add one (see "Logging in to your Media profile" below). Your location is used only on your phone, when you press SOS or "Share my location" (see "Family & SOS" below); it is never sent to our server.

When you send a message, the text of your message and the recent conversation is sent to our server and then to Anthropic's Claude AI service so it can write a reply. If you tap "Report" on a reply, we save that reply, your question and your reason so we can improve the app.

We do not store your conversations on our server.

Our server keeps short-lived technical records (such as your internet address) only to stop misuse and too many requests.

Who processes your messages

Replies are generated by Claude, an AI service from Anthropic. Anthropic processes messages under its own commercial terms and privacy policy and does not use API messages to train its models by default. Read more at anthropic.com/legal/privacy.

Backup AI. If Anthropic's service cannot answer (for example during an outage), the same request may be sent instead to Google's Gemini API, as a backup only. We use Gemini only as a paid service, under which Google does not use the requests to improve its products. Read more at ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms.

When no AI can be reached. If there is no internet or no AI service can answer, a small helper inside the app handles simple requests (such as reminders, alarms, timers, calls to your family, opening apps, your list, the time, sums and the phone's own message check) on your phone, without sending anything.

Looking things up without the AI. If the AI cannot answer and you ask for the weather, the news or what something is, the app asks these public services directly from your phone: Open-Meteo for the weather (the town you name, or, only if you already allowed location for Family & SOS, your rough area to about 10 km), Google News for headlines, and Wikipedia for short explanations. Only the words of your question are sent to them, nothing else about you.

Voice and microphone

The app uses the microphone only while you tap the puppy to talk. Your phone's own voice-typing service turns your speech into text; we receive only that text, never audio. Replies are read aloud by your phone's text-to-speech engine.

Natural voice

If natural voice is on, the text the pet speaks out loud (its replies and reminders, and the messages it reads aloud if you turn on "Read new messages aloud") is sent from our server to Google Cloud Text-to-Speech to create the spoken audio. Your own voice recordings are never sent. The audio files are kept on our server for up to 7 days so repeated phrases play faster, then deleted. You can switch natural voice off in the app; the pet then uses your phone's own voice.

Photos

If you choose a photo to ask about, a smaller copy is sent through our server to Anthropic's Claude service to answer your question. We do not store your photos.

Our own brain (when the main AI can't answer)

If Claude can't answer (it is down, there is no key, or the day's budget is used up), and Gemini can't either, an open AI model that runs on our own server answers instead (Google's Gemma, run with Ollama). Your message, and a photo if you sent one, are then used only on our server and not sent to any AI company. To answer, the server may look up the news (Google News), Wikipedia and the weather (Open-Meteo) with words from your question, never with who you are. Nothing is stored for this.

The tiny brain on your phone (Habit Brain, optional)

Habit Brain is a separate, optional app with a small AI model (Google's Gemma 4) that runs on your phone. When there is no internet, Habit AI asks it, and what you ask stays on your phone. The model (about 2.6 GB) is downloaded once, only when you press Download in Habit Brain, from Hugging Face (or a place the app owner names); the download itself sends nothing about you. Only apps signed by us can ask it.

Learning from common questions

To answer questions many people ask (for example "What is PM Kisan?") quickly, our server counts general questions. Questions about yourself (with words like "I", "my", "నా", "నేను"), about today, the news or prices, with long numbers or links, greetings and commands are never kept. We don't keep who asked: only a one-way mark that changes every month, so the same phone isn't counted twice. When several different people ask the same question, a short answer is prepared with Claude and the internet and kept for everyone; questions only one or two people asked are deleted after 30 days. The app owner can see and delete these questions and answers. You can turn this off in the app: Settings › Privacy › "Help Habit learn common questions".

The pet's eyes (looking when you ask)

When you ask your pet to look at something ("look at this shirt", "what is written on this board?", "translate this", "what colour is my shirt?"), and only after you say yes the first time (and Android asks for the camera permission), the pet takes one photo with the back camera (for things in front of you) or the front camera (for you). No camera window opens: the app shows a small note "Looking…" and Android shows its green camera dot. A smaller copy of that photo, without hidden details such as the place it was taken, is sent through our server to Anthropic's Claude service (or, only if Claude cannot answer, to Google's Gemini service) only to answer your question; for questions such as "is this available online?" Claude may also search the internet. We do not store the photo, and it is not kept on your phone. The camera is closed right after. Apart from the optional look when you open the app (below), the pet looks only when you ask. If the AI cannot be reached, the phone itself may read the English letters in that photo (Google's on-phone text reader) and nothing is sent.

Pet camera

Only if you say yes (the app asks first, and Android asks for the camera permission), your pet can take a quick look at you with the front camera: no camera window opens, the app shows a note "Looking at you…" and Android shows its green camera dot, and after about 2 seconds it takes one photo. This happens when you open the app (at most once every 3 hours) or when you ask ("look at me"). A smaller copy of that photo is sent through our server to Anthropic's Claude service (or, only if Claude cannot answer, to Google's Gemini service) to say something kind and give a tip. We do not store the photo, and it is not kept on your phone. The camera is closed right after. You can turn this off at any time on the Pet page.

Finding cards for your Vault

In Habit AI Lite, if you turn on "Find my cards automatically" in the Vault and allow Photos, the app looks at your screenshots and WhatsApp pictures by itself (the newest 400 the first time, then new ones only while the phone is charging). In the Play Store edition, you choose the pictures yourself. Either way they are read on your phone only: Google's on-phone text reader (part of Google Play services) reads the words and the app reads Aadhaar QR codes itself. Nothing is sent to our server or to any AI. The cards it finds are saved, with their photos, encrypted in your Vault on the phone. Bank cards are not added this way.

Lists, notes, reminders and memories

Your to-do list, notes, reminders and the things the puppy remembers about you are saved only on your phone. With each message, the app sends your to-do list, notes and memories to our server so the AI can use them in its answer; the good-morning briefing also sends today's reminders. We do not store them on our server. Medicine reminders you add on the Family & SOS page (💊) are never sent: they stay on your phone and are read aloud by your phone's own voice. You can delete any of them in the app at any time.

Contacts

If you allow Contacts, the app looks up a phone number on your phone when you ask it to call or message someone. When you add a family member from Contacts on the Family & SOS page, Android shares only the one contact you pick. Your contacts are never sent to our server.

Internet search

For questions about current events, prices or other recent information, the AI may search the web. The search is done by Anthropic's service using words from your question; links to the sources are shown with the answer.

Message replies (WhatsApp, SMS and other chat apps)

This feature is off until you turn it on and give the app "Notification access" in your phone's settings. When it is on, the app reads new message notifications only from the chat apps you choose in the app (for example WhatsApp or Messages). For each new message, the sender's name, the chat name and the message text are sent to our server and to Anthropic's Claude service to write a suggested reply. Nothing is sent to the other person until you tap Send, unless you switch on Auto-reply.

If you switch on Auto-reply, the app replies by itself to new messages from the chosen apps, based on the auto-reply message you write, and stops when you switch it off. It does not auto-reply in group chats and replies at most once per chat every 30 minutes. We do not store your messages or replies on our server. You can turn the feature off in the app, or remove Notification access in your phone's settings, at any time.

Message safety check (fraud and danger alerts)

You can paste or share a message into the app to check whether it is safe. When automatic checking is on (Play Store edition, with Notification access as described above), every new message from the chat apps you choose is first checked on your phone for warning signs such as links, requests for OTPs, PINs or money, or threats. Only messages with warning signs are sent, with the sender's name or number and the app's name, to our server and to Anthropic's Claude service to judge whether they are safe, need care, or are dangerous. We do not store these messages on our server. The list of checked messages and their safety labels is saved only on your phone for 7 days and then removed by itself; you can remove one message, or the whole list, on the Messages page at any time.

Checking a screenshot or QR code. If you choose a screenshot or take a photo to check, a smaller copy of the picture (with hidden details such as the place it was taken removed) is sent through our server to Anthropic's Claude service only to check it. We do not store these pictures. A QR code in the picture is read on your phone itself; what it contains (for example a UPI payment request) is sent along with the picture to help the check.

Explain, reply and report. When you tap Explain or Write the reply for a message, its text (and the sender's name, for a reply) is sent through our server to Anthropic's Claude service to explain it in simple words or to suggest a reply; nothing is stored on our server. A reply is sent only when you send it yourself in WhatsApp, SMS or another app, or when you tap Send and confirm. "Ask my family" and "Police complaint" only open WhatsApp, SMS or the letter helper with the message filled in; you decide whether to send it.

This week's new scams. The list of new frauds is made on our server from public news reports, using Anthropic's Claude service with web search, and is the same for everyone. Your phone downloads it when you open the Messages page (at most every 12 hours); nothing about you or your messages is used to make it.

Letter helper (Public and Office)

When you make a letter or document, your request and your answers to the helper's questions (typed or spoken) are sent to our server and to Anthropic's Claude service to write the document. In Office mode, the office details you save (designation, office, district, and optionally the officer's name, the file-number style and an office phone or email) stay on your phone and are sent along with each office document. The finished Word or Excel file is kept on our server for up to 24 hours so your phone can download it, and is then deleted automatically. If "Hide personal details" is on, Aadhaar, phone and PAN numbers in what you type are masked before they leave your phone.

If you add papers about the matter (photos you take or choose, or a PDF), they are sent to our server and to Anthropic's Claude service so the AI can read them and write the document. Photos are made smaller on your phone first, which also removes hidden details such as the location. Our server keeps the papers only until the document is made, at most 6 hours, and then deletes them; they are not included in our backups. "Hide personal details" cannot mask what is inside photos and PDFs, so please don't add papers you are not allowed to share.

Translate

When you translate, the text you type, paste or say, or the photo you take or choose (for example of a signboard, letter or notice), is sent to our server and to Anthropic's Claude service to read and translate it. We do not store your text, photos or translations. Photos are made smaller on your phone first, which also removes hidden details such as the location. If "Hide personal details" is on, Aadhaar, phone and PAN numbers in typed text are masked before they leave your phone. Spoken words are turned into text by your phone's voice typing service, and translations are read aloud by your phone's own voice. Photos you take for translating are saved inside the app only for a moment and are deleted right after.

Government services

Habit AI is not a government app and is not linked to any government. The Govt services page shows links to official government websites. When you ask a question there, your question and the state you chose (Andhra Pradesh or Telangana) are sent to our server and to Anthropic's Claude service, which searches only official government websites (gov.in and nic.in) and answers with links to them. We do not store your questions. Please check details on the official website or at the government office.

Family & SOS

Family members you add (name, phone number and relation) are saved only on your phone, never on our server. When you press SOS or "Share my location", and only if you allowed location, the app gets your current location from your phone and puts a Google Maps link in the message that you choose to send by SMS, WhatsApp or another app. Your location is used only at that moment: it is not sent to our server or to the AI, and it is not saved. If you allow calling, SOS calls your first family member after a 5-second countdown that you can cancel; the app makes calls only when you press SOS or a call button. Emergency numbers (such as 112) open your phone app with the number ready. You can remove family members, and turn off location or calling in your phone's settings, at any time.

Media page

The Media page shows short posts (news, movies, jobs, devotional, farming, health, kids' stories and more) written by AI from public websites, each with a link to its source, and links to YouTube videos. To show a post's picture, the app loads it directly from the website it comes from (for example a news site, or YouTube for video pictures), so that website can see your internet address, as with any web page. Videos you play inside the app are played by YouTube. Opening a link or video takes you to that website or to YouTube, whose own privacy policies apply. When you like a post, our server stores the like with a random id linked to your app (not your name or phone number). Reading the posts aloud uses your phone's own voice. For some posts our server also makes a short video (the post's picture and text, with music) that you can share; it contains only the public post, nothing about you.

Profiles, posts, photos and comments (Media tab)

If you make a profile, your name, @name, tagline and profile photo are public. Posts, photos, links and comments you share in the Media tab are public too: everyone using Habit AI can see them, together with your profile. Before anything appears, the text is checked automatically, and every photo is checked by Anthropic's Claude service for unsafe content (nudity, violence, ID cards and similar); photos that fail are not stored. Photos are re-saved on your phone before upload, which removes hidden details such as the location. Videos you post (up to 1 minute) are made smaller on our server, which also removes hidden details such as the place they were filmed, and a few still pictures from each video are checked by Anthropic's Claude service before anyone can see it; videos that fail the check are deleted, and uploaded videos that are never posted are deleted within 48 hours. For links, our server reads the page's title and preview picture to show a preview. Your profile is linked to a random id of the app on your phone, not to your phone number; a backup code lets you move it to a new phone (keep it private). Likes, follows, views and reports are stored with your profile id. You can delete your posts and comments at any time, and your whole profile (see "Deleting your profile" below). When people report a post or comment, we keep the report and its reason until our team has checked it. See also our community rules and child safety standards.

Logging in to your Media profile

If you choose to, you can add your mobile number or email address and a password to your Media profile, so you can open the profile on a new phone. We keep the number or email only to log you in: it is never shown to other people, not used for marketing and not shared. We do not send you SMS or emails. Your password is never stored as you typed it: we keep only a scrambled form of it (a scrypt hash) that cannot be turned back into the password. You can change or remove the login at any time (Media › Edit profile), and it is deleted with your profile.

Posts from the earlier Community page

Earlier versions of the app had a Community page. Posts shared there are public and are now shown in the People section of the Media tab, under the display name chosen at the time. They are stored with a scrambled code made from the app's random device id (not a name, phone number or account). Posts that break the community rules are removed. Deleting your profile from the app also deletes the posts, likes and reports that your phone made on the Community page.

Reading your screen (only when you ask)

If you tap "Read my screen" or ask the pet about your screen, Android first asks your permission ("Start capturing?"). Only after you agree, the app takes one picture of your screen, and a smaller copy is sent through our server to Anthropic's Claude service to answer your question. The picture is deleted from the phone right after it is sent, and we do not store it on our server. The app never records your screen continuously. Screens that block screenshots, such as banking apps, password screens and the app's own Vault, appear black.

Hands-free control (Habit AI Plus only)

The Plus edition can use Android's Accessibility service, only if you turn it on after reading an explanation in the app. It performs a fixed action when you ask for it: when you say a command such as "next reel", "pause", "go back" or "go home", or tap one of the pet's remote buttons, it performs exactly that one swipe, tap or button press. It does not read, collect, store or send the content of your screen, and the AI never decides to act on its own. In "Reels voice mode" the pet keeps listening only for the words next, previous, pause and like; what you say is turned into text by your phone's voice typing service, and the words are checked on your phone only, never sent to our server. You can switch it off at any time in your phone's Accessibility settings.

Floating on your screen

With your permission the pet appears over other apps. Apart from the message notifications described above, the app cannot see or read what other apps show.

Habit Chat (messages, groups, calls, status updates)

Habit Chat messages, photos, voice notes, files and status updates are locked end to end on your phone before they leave it: only the people in the chat can open them, not our server. Our server keeps a locked message only until every device of every person in the chat has received it (at most 30 days), and locked files for at most 30 days. To pass messages on, the server knows your profile name and @name, your chat lock's public key, who is in which chat, when messages were delivered and read (the ticks), and when you were last online (you can hide this in Chat settings). Voice and video calls go directly between the two phones, locked; when they can't reach each other, our relay passes the locked call on without being able to open it.

Habit Chat on a computer (bigdai.tech/web)

You can link up to 4 computers to your phone by scanning a QR code shown at bigdai.tech/web. Your phone then sends that computer its chat key and a copy of your recent chats, locked for that QR code only, so our server can't open them. Locked chats, your notes, the Habit AI chat, broadcast lists and view-once photos stay on your phone. For each linked computer the server keeps a random device code, the browser's name (for example "Chrome on Windows") and when it was last used. The computer keeps your chats in that browser; if you untick "Keep me signed in", nothing is kept and you are logged out when you close the tab. You can log a computer out from your phone (Chats › ⋮ › Linked devices) or from the computer; a computer not used for 30 days, and every computer when your phone gets a new chat lock, is logged out by itself.

Children

Habit AI is made for adults (18 and over) and is not directed at children. The Kids section of the Media tab has stories and rhymes for parents to read to their children.

AI replies can be wrong

Habit AI is an AI and can make mistakes, including in translations. Please check important information before you rely on it.

Deleting your profile

You can delete your Media profile at any time: in the app (Media › your picture › Edit profile › Delete my profile, or Pet page › Privacy › Delete my Media profile), or on the web at bigdai.tech/delete-account with your login or your backup code, or by email to bigdai.new@gmail.com. This deletes your profile (name, @name, tagline, photo, backup code and login), all your posts and their photos and videos, comments on your posts, your comments, likes, follows and reports, and the link between your phone and the profile. Copies in our automatic nightly backups are deleted within 7 days. If our team had stopped a profile for breaking the rules, we keep a scrambled code of the phone so that the same phone can't simply start posting again.

Deleting your information

Uninstalling the app removes everything it saved on your phone. To ask us to delete any other information, such as reports you sent about an AI answer, email bigdai.new@gmail.com.
